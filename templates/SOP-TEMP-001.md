# Standard Operating Procedure: Document Control and Data Logging

**Document ID:** SOP-QC-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** June 19, 2026  

### 1. Purpose
To define the procedure for logging analytical data within the Electronic Laboratory Notebook to ensure strict compliance with data integrity guidelines.

### 2. Scope
Applies to all computational raw data, instrumentation calibrations, and analytical results generated during research phases.

### 3. Procedure
1. All analytical observations must be documented immediately upon completion of the execution.
2. Raw data from instruments (e.g., UV-Vis Spectrophotometer, Potentiometers) must be recorded with exact instrument serial numbers and software versions used.
3. Any modifications to existing files must be documented with a clear justification in the commit log to preserve the audit trail.
