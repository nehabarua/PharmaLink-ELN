# Comparative Translational Analysis: Acetaminophen vs. Ibuprofen

## Project Overview
This project maps the structural biochemistry and preclinical translational pathways of classic non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) and analgesics, focusing on Cyclooxygenase (COX) enzymatic selectivity.

## 1. Structural Binding & Target Coordinates
Analysis of human COX enzyme variants utilized for molecular docking reference:
* **Target 1:** Human Cyclooxygenase-1 (PDB ID: `1EQG`)
* **Target 2:** Human Cyclooxygenase-2 (PDB ID: `1CX2`)

### Active Site Amino Acid Interactions
* **Ibuprofen Pathway:** Exhibits competitive, non-selective inhibition across both COX-1 and COX-2 active sites, interacting directly with residues `Arg120` and `Tyr355`.
* **Acetaminophen Pathway:** Shows central nervous system dominance; interaction is highly dependent on cellular redox states and peroxide concentrations.

## 2. Translational Medicine & Clinical Outcomes
| Compound | Preclinical Mechanism | Bedside/Clinical Manifestation | Secondary Metabolic Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ibuprofen** | Peripheral COX-1 Inhibition | Gastric Mucosal Depletion | Increased Risk of GI Ulceration |
| **Acetaminophen** | Central COX Inhibition | Analgesia without Anti-inflammatory effect | Hepatic Glutathione Depletion via NAPQI |

## 3. Computational Methods Used
* Structural visualization via Protein Data Bank (RCSB PDB).
* Target sequence alignment and ligand binding analysis.
